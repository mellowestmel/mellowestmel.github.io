The TBB:TARPG Archive

This page archives screenshots and footage from when TBB:TARPG was still public, as well as other information documenting the game's history.
It is important to note that my username used to be "shibathedeveloper" (and/or variations of it).

Versions/Eras

TBB:TARPG went through four major versions throughout its lifetime. Each version was significantly different from the previous one, and each had an internal nickname used during development.

Note: some of the code (primarily in V3-V4) produces errors in the studio/gameplay footage I recorded. It used to work when the game was public.

V1: "The Grey Box"

The original version of TBB:TARPG. The entire reason this game was made was because two people asked me to "PLEASE MAKE A TBB RP GAME!!".
I won't say who those two people were, but one of them ended up cheating in the Duck Trials in V3, which was pretty funny.

V1 was extremely bare-bones compared to later versions. It lasted for around 300 game versions before being replaced by V2.

Info about what "The Duck Trials" are is in the V3 section.

Studio/Gameplay footage: https://youtu.be/ty5DvEnu_sA

V2: "The Beach"

V2 stuck around for a long time, lasting around 1,100 game versions.
It was made relatively quickly because I wanted to get a new version out as soon as possible, while the TBB:TARPG community was becoming increasingly impatient for new content.

The version originally released without Tumore Mode, which upset a lot of impatient players.
The amount of pressure I received to release new content during this period was significant, and this ultimately contributed to Chapter 2 being released in an unfinished and broken state.

Studio/Gameplay footage: https://youtu.be/FEaEOjBkLpc

V3: "The Valley"

After the problems with V2's Chapter 2, I knew I had to do something different.
V3 took a very long time to release, despite the community becoming increasingly impatient for it.

Within the development team, V3 was more commonly referred to as "shit client code". At the time, the "dev team" consisted of me, Noah, and Gochged.
BAHAHTYNK later joined and worked on the Chapter 2 maps and one Coil Chaos map, but their stay was relatively short-lived.
They were eventually replaced by bosswhyth, who went on to make a large number of high-quality maps for TBB:TARPG.

Studio/Gameplay footage: https://youtu.be/pXIgrdpW_b8

V3 Additional Info: The Duck Trials

The Duck Trials were an obby in V3 which was accessed by writing "I wish to enter the Duck Trials." in chat. It was made because a pretty big donator wanted to make their custom cosmetic available to everybody. They said we could make it obtainable in whatever way we wanted, so of course we settled for a REALLY annoying and hard obstacle course.

The Duck Trials had two versions. You can play the oldest version here, while the newest version can be played here.

V4: "The Good One"

The fourth and final major version of TBB:TARPG.

It was never finished due to major burnout affecting both me and the animator. As its name suggests, it was the most advanced version of TBB:TARPG.

I also lazily add a pop-up telling you to follow ROBLOX TOS, because some people apparently didn't know you had to do that. Some dude and his friends complained that he got banned for roleplaying going onto a "BloxHub" (ew)

Studio/Gameplay footage: https://youtu.be/4rUBmZsZu7g
